1. Run a Notify instance (its database and object store are yours; the
   Secrets it names carry the label notify.weebo.io/credential: "true"):

     kubectl -n team apply -f - <<YAML
     apiVersion: notify.weebo.io/v1alpha1
     kind: NotifyLocalInstance
     metadata:
       name: notify
     spec:
       database:
         urlSecretRef: {name: notify-db, key: url}
       attachments:
         s3:
           endpoint: http://rustfs:9000
           bucket: notify
           credentialsSecretRef: {name: notify-s3}
       oidc:
         issuer: https://id.example.com
         clientId: notify
     YAML

2. Then declare its NotifyChannel, NotifyServiceAccount, NotifyTransform
   and NotifyTrustPolicy resources (`spec.instanceRef` unset: the only
   instance of their namespace).
